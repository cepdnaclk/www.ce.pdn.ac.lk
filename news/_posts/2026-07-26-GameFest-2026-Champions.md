<p dir="ltr">A team of undergraduate students from PeraCom, the <a href="https://www.ce.pdn.ac.lk/">Department of Computer Engineering</a>, University of Peradeniya, emerged as Champions in the University Category at GameFest 2026, an eight-hour game development competition organized by SLIIT Kandy, held on 24 July 2026 at the SLIIT Kandy campus in Pallekele.</p>
<p dir="ltr">GameFest 2026 brought together talented university and school teams to compete in a fast-paced game development challenge, where participants were required to conceptualize, design, and develop a complete game within just eight hours. The University Category featured five competing teams, while several school teams also participated, showcasing creativity, technical skills, and innovation in game development.</p>
<p dir="ltr">Representing the University of Peradeniya, the PeraCom team secured First Place in the University Category with a top-down time-management survival game developed around the competition theme, "Extinction." The game challenges players to gather resources during the day to build and maintain a bonfire, enabling them to survive relentless monster attacks throughout the night. The project demonstrated the team's creativity, game design skills, and ability to deliver a polished and engaging gameplay experience under intense time constraints.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The winning team comprised <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/050/">Thakshila Bandara</a>, <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/049/">Lahiru Dinushan</a>, <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/077/">Shamishka Dharshana</a>, and <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/024/">Induwara Amaaranga</a>, undergraduate students from the E21 batch of the Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya.</p>
<p dir="ltr">This outstanding achievement highlights the team's creativity, technical expertise, and collaborative spirit in game development. Competing against talented teams within a limited development timeframe, the students demonstrated exceptional problem-solving skills and innovation to deliver a compelling game experience.</p>
<p dir="ltr">Congratulations to the team on this remarkable accomplishment and for bringing pride and recognition to the Department of Computer Engineering and the University of Peradeniya.</p>
