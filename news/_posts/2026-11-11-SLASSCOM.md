<p dir="ltr">Team Spectraleaf, representing the <a href="https://www.ce.pdn.ac.lk/">Department of Computer Engineering,</a> University of Peradeniya, secured third place (second runner-up) at the Xcellerate Inter-University Hackathon 2026. Organized by SLASSCOM, the event took place on 5 November 2026 at the Sheraton Colombo Hotel in Colombo 3.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The hackathon drew more than 150 teams, giving university students an opportunity to apply their technical skills and work together to solve problems. Team Spectraleaf earned a place on the podium in a field of more than 150 teams.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The team comprised <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/226/">Nadeera Kothalawala</a>, <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/049/">Lahiru Dinushan</a>, <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/200/">Rangana Madhushanka</a>, and <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/054/">Deshan Dinidu.</a> The project was carried out under the supervision and guidance of<a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/staff/academic/yasodha-vimukthi/"> Ms. Yasodha Vimukthi,</a><a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/staff/academic/isuru-nawinne/"> Dr. Isuru Nawinne</a>, and<a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/staff/academic/thilina-gunarathne/"> Mr.&nbsp; Thilina Gunarathne.</a>&nbsp;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Congratulations to Team Spectraleaf on this achievement and for bringing recognition to the Department of Computer Engineering and the University of Peradeniya.</p>
