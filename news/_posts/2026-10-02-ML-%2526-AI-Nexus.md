<p dir="ltr">Team யாழி, comprising undergraduates from the <a href="https://www.ce.pdn.ac.lk/">Department of Computer Engineering</a>, University of Peradeniya, secured first runner-up at ML &amp; AI Nexus, an AI and machine learning competition organized by the Department of Statistics, University of Colombo.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The competition brought together 17 teams from state and private institutions, with four teams advancing to the final held on 28 September 2026. Team யாழி earned a place among the finalists before achieving second place overall.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The team comprised <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/138/">&nbsp;Fikry Nuhuman</a>, <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/148/">Ganathipan Sivasakthynathan</a>, <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/188/">Jarshigan Kamalanathan</a>, and <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/220/">Kavishanthan Sarvaloganayakam.</a></p>
<p dir="ltr">Congratulations to Team யாழி for proudly representing PeraCom at ML &amp; AI Nexus. This achievement reflects their dedication and teamwork, and we look forward to seeing their continued growth in AI and machine learning.</p>
