<p dir="ltr">Team ResQ from the<a href="https://www.ce.pdn.ac.lk/"> Department of Computer Engineering</a>, University of Peradeniya, placed third in the Biomedical Engineering and Emerging Technologies category at IEEE FYP Arena 2026, held on 3 October 2026.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The competition brought together students from state and non-state universities across Sri Lanka to showcase their final-year projects.</p>
<p dir="ltr">Team ResQ presented a smart CPR training system that uses sensors fitted to a training manikin to measure chest compression depth, rate, and recoil. The system provides real-time feedback so trainees can adjust their technique during practice and review their performance afterwards.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The team comprised<a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/148/"> S. Ganathipan</a>,<a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/152/"> V. Amirsha</a>,<a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/214/"> K. Kartheepan</a>, and<a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e21/220/"> S. Kavishanthan</a>, all undergraduates in the Department of Computer Engineering. The project was carried out under the supervision and guidance of <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/staff/academic/yasodha-vimukthi/">Ms. Yasodha Vimukthi,</a> <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/staff/academic/isuru-nawinne/">Dr. Isuru Nawinne</a>, and <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/staff/academic/thilina-gunarathne/">Mr.&nbsp; Thilina Gunarathne.</a></p>
<p dir="ltr">The Department congratulates Team ResQ on its podium finish and looks forward to seeing how the team further develops the system to support CPR training.</p>
