<p dir="ltr">A team of undergraduate students from PeraCom, the <a href="https://www.ce.pdn.ac.lk/">Department of Computer Engineering</a>, University of Peradeniya, emerged as Champions at the NeuroX Buildathon 2026, organized by the Hackathon Hub of NSBM, held on 31 July 2026.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The NeuroX Buildathon attracted 80 teams during its initial proposal submission phase, from which only 15 teams were shortlisted to compete in the onsite finals. The final round featured live product demonstrations and pitch presentations before an experienced panel of industry experts, including Mr. Shamal De Silva, CTO and Co-founder of Cue Labs.</p>
<p dir="ltr">Representing the University of Peradeniya, Team Alt+F4 secured the Championship by presenting "Marga," an agentic B2B solution designed for supply chain companies. The platform continuously scans news sources for potential disruptions, intelligently analyzes their impact, identifies alternative routes, and recommends the most optimal logistics paths for operators and shipping companies. The solution showcased the team's innovation, technical expertise, and ability to address real-world supply chain challenges using intelligent automation.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The winning team comprised <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e22/405/">Daniel Victor</a>, <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e22/151/">Imaadh Ifthikar</a>, <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e22/056/">Daniru Dinsara</a>, and <a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/students/e22/180/">Geethma Chandoopa</a>, undergraduate students from the E22 batch of the Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya.</p>
<p dir="ltr">This outstanding achievement highlights the team's creativity, technical excellence, and entrepreneurial mindset in developing impactful technology solutions. Competing against some of the country's most promising student innovators, the team demonstrated exceptional problem-solving, presentation, and product development skills to emerge as champions.</p>
<p dir="ltr">Congratulations to Team Alt+F4 on this remarkable accomplishment and for bringing pride and recognition to the Department of Computer Engineering and University of Peradeniya.</p>
