<p dir="ltr"><a href="https://people.ce.pdn.ac.lk/staff/academic/roshan-ragel/">Prof. Roshan G. Ragel</a> of the Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya, received the CSSL National ICT Educator of the Year award at the CSSL National ICT Awards 2025, presented during NITC 2026 by the Computer Society of Sri Lanka.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The award recognises his outstanding contributions to computing education, his commitment to mentoring generations of engineers, and his impact on Sri Lanka&rsquo;s ICT community through teaching, research, and industry collaboration.</p>
<p dir="ltr">This national recognition marks a proud milestone for Prof. Ragel and brings honour to the Department of Computer Engineering, the Faculty of Engineering, and the University of Peradeniya. His dedication to education continues to inspire students and colleagues alike.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The Department of Computer Engineering extends its warmest congratulations to Prof. Roshan G. Ragel on this achievement and wishes him continued success in his contributions to education and the ICT sector.</p>
